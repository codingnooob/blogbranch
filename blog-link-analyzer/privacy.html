Privacy Policy for Blog Link Analyzer Extension

Last Updated: January 16, 2025
Effective Date: January 16, 2025

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the Blog Link Analyzer browser extension ("the Extension") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our service.

Information We Collect

1. Automatically Collected Information

Blog Content Analysis

  • The Extension analyzes web pages you visit to detect blog posts and extract links to other blog posts
  • Page content is processed locally in your browser to identify blog posts and extract metadata (titles, authors)
  • No page content is sent to external servers unless you explicitly use AI features

Extension Usage Data

  • Local storage of extension settings and preferences
  • Cached blog post metadata for performance optimization
  • AI configuration settings (if enabled)

2. AI Feature Data (Optional)

When you enable AI summarization features:

  • Blog post content may be sent to AI service providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Ollama, or custom endpoints) based on your configuration
  • API keys are stored locally in your browser and sent directly to the chosen AI provider
  • Summary results are cached locally in your browser for performance

How We Use Your Information

Primary Uses

  • Blog Detection: Identify blog posts on web pages you visit
  • Link Analysis: Extract and organize links to other blog posts
  • AI Summarization: Generate summaries of blog content (when explicitly requested)
  • Performance: Cache data to improve extension performance

No Third-Party Sharing

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes
  • No data is sent to external servers except AI providers you explicitly configure

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

  • All extension data is stored locally using browser storage APIs
  • Data includes: settings, preferences, cached blog metadata, and AI configuration
  • You can clear all extension data through browser settings or extension options

AI API Keys

  • API keys are stored locally and encrypted where browser support is available
  • Keys are sent directly to your chosen AI provider; we never intercept or store them
  • You are responsible for securing your API keys

Data Retention

  • Extension data is stored until you clear it or uninstall the extension
  • AI summary caches can be cleared through extension settings
  • No long-term data retention on external servers

Your Rights and Choices

Control Over Your Data

  • Disable AI Features: Turn off AI summarization in extension settings
  • Clear Data: Clear cached data and settings through extension options
  • Uninstall: Remove the extension completely to delete all stored data

Transparency

  • All data processing happens locally unless AI features are used
  • You can see exactly what data is being processed through browser developer tools
  • Extension is open source; code is available for review

AI Service Providers

When you use AI features, your chosen provider's privacy policy applies:

  • OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/privacy
  • Ollama: Local processing only (no external data transmission)
  • Custom Endpoints: Privacy policy depends on your chosen service

Children's Privacy

The Extension is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

International Data Transfers

When you use AI features, data may be transferred to countries where your chosen AI provider operates. By enabling AI features, you consent to such transfers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Posting in-extension notifications for significant changes
  • Providing in-extension notification with a link to view the updated policy

Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices:

  • GitHub Issues: https://github.com/your-repo/blog-link-analyzer/issues
  • Email: blog-link-analyzer@example.com

Compliance

This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with:

  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
  • Chrome Web Store Policies
  • Firefox Add-on Guidelines

Summary: The Blog Link Analyzer processes blog content locally in your browser. Only when you explicitly enable AI features does content get sent to external providers. All data is stored locally and under your control.
